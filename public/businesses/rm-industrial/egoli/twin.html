    Operational boundaries

    • 15-minute simulation; weekday operating windows; upstream 06–22, finishing 07–15.
    • Rates are bottle equivalents/hour, not litres. Tanks are aggregate compatible capacity; actual vessel assignment, CIP and recipe routing are not modelled.
    • One synthetic product. No ingredient-level BOM, ABV/temperature physics, energy balance, workforce sharing or financial cash-flow model.
    • QA releases one eligible lot per window. A hold stays quarantined until an explicit simulated decision after 24 hours.
    • Bottling rejects consume bulk and kits. Packed good units are assumed final-released; a separate finished-goods inspection is not modelled.
    • Orders cancel 14 days after their due date, by assumption. On-time-in-full uses orders due within the last 7 days.
    • Constraint markers are persistent queue-pressure hypotheses, not proof of root cause or investment recommendations.
    • No sensor, ERP, lab or customer connection. Geometry and movement are illustrative and are not physical simulation.
